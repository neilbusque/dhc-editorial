Ask every contractor you interview the following six questions. The ones who
answer with specifics — not slogans — are the ones to keep talking to.

1. **How many active jobs are you running right now?**
2. **Who is the lead on my job? Is it the same person from demo to punch list?**
3. **What's your change-order process?** (If they don't have one, walk.)
4. **Can I visit a project you finished two years ago — not last month?**
5. **What's your warranty on labor?**
6. **Show me a project that went sideways. What did you do about it?**

That last one is the tell. The good ones have a story ready.
