The honest answer is that we can't keep our quality up past six.

A whole-home renovation has roughly 800 decisions. A kitchen has around 200. If
you're juggling twelve jobs, you're touching one of them every other day. Errors
compound, suppliers slip, the same finisher visits five sites in one week and
nobody catches the off-batch grout until it's already on the floor.

We've run it the other way. It doesn't work — at least not for the kind of work
we want to put our name on.
