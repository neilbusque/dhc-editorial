The most expensive tile mistake is also the most common: ordering the exact
square footage the floor plan calls for. We order 15% extra. Always.

Here's why: tile breaks in transit, breaks during cut, breaks five years later
when a hairline fault opens up under a heavy step. If you don't have backup
stock in the basement, you're matching a dye lot that no longer exists. That's
a thousand-dollar problem on a four-cent tile.

A few more things we've learned the hard way:

- **Never mix dye lots within a single field.** Always stage tiles from
  multiple boxes before laying — the human eye picks up batch variance instantly
  on a floor, almost never on a wall.
- **Don't grout for 72 hours.** Some installers do 24. Then the grout cracks
  three months in. Patience is free.
- **For showers, always Schluter or Wedi.** Cement board is fine for floors.
  In a shower, it fails eventually.
