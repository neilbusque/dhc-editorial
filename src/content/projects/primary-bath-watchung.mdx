The homeowners had lived in the house for nine years and had bathed in the
master tub exactly zero times. We pulled it out.

What replaced it: a 5×7 walk-in shower with a curbless entry, heated polished
limestone floors, and a 12-foot vanity wall built around one piece of Calacatta
we hand-picked from a Brooklyn yard. The slab cost more than the cabinetry. The
homeowners cried when it landed. So did we, a little.

## The build

- Demoed to studs; rewired, replumbed, re-vented to current code
- Schluter Ditra under everything that gets wet
- Linear drain along the inside wall (not the outside — the slope is invisible)
- Wall-hung double vanity, brass legs, no toe-kick
- Brizo Litze fixtures in luxe gold

## What we learned

If your client has never used the tub, **do not put in a new tub**. Build the
room they will actually live in.
