When a 1908 Colonial in Plainfield's historic district came back on the market
after sitting empty for a year, the new owners called us with a question:
"Can this be brought back without ruining it?"

Yes. Carefully.

## Scope

- Eight months on-site
- Four bedrooms, two full baths, one half bath
- Full kitchen reworked (no addition — we kept the footprint)
- New windows, custom-ordered to match historical sash
- Every piece of crown, base, and casing in the house hand-milled to match the original profile
- Original quartersawn oak floors sanded, not replaced
- Roof, electric panel, plumbing risers all renewed

## Crew

One lead carpenter from start to punch list. Same plumber every visit. Same
finisher. That's how you keep this kind of job from going sideways.

## Lessons

A whole-home is not three kitchens stacked on top of each other. The sequencing
is the project. We rough-framed every room before any tile went down, on purpose.
It pushed completion out four weeks. It also meant zero rework.
